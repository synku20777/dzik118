## 1. What this admin must see

The dashboard should answer one question first: **“What must I do next to finish this month?”** The documented monthly cycle is readings → invoice generation → preparation/delivery → reconciliation, with the dashboard intended for monitoring and prioritization and the workbench for execution (`docs/product/USER_JOURNEYS.md:7`, `docs/product/SERVICE_DESIGN_BLUEPRINT.md:97`).

It therefore must show:

- **Month and deadlines:** selected billing month, OPEN/LOCKED state, reading deadline, issue date and payment due date (`docs/product/SERVICE_DESIGN_BLUEPRINT.md:36`, `docs/research/EASYBILLY_REVIEW_2026-10-05.md:117`).
- **Reading completeness:** dwellings complete/total, dwellings missing data, and actual missing readings/manual inputs—not merely a status label (`src/domain/periods/case-readiness.ts:21`, `src/domain/periods/case-readiness.ts:28`, `src/domain/periods/case-readiness.ts:200`).
- **Invoice readiness and progress:** how many can be invoiced now, remain blocked, are drafts, are prepared, were sent, failed delivery, or are complete (`docs/product/SERVICE_DESIGN_BLUEPRINT.md:35`, `docs/product/SERVICE_DESIGN_BLUEPRINT.md:154`).
- **Financial position with reconcilable definitions:** current-month charges, debt/credit carried forward, total due, paid, remaining, and overdue. Partial payments must retain a remaining balance; overpayments become account credit (`docs/product/USER_JOURNEYS.md:46`, `docs/product/USER_JOURNEYS.md:78`).
- **Actionable exceptions:** dwelling, amount, due date, cause and next action for overdue invoices, delivery failures, unmatched payments and unusual readings (`docs/product/USER_JOURNEYS.md:63`, `docs/product/USER_JOURNEYS.md:82`, `docs/product/SERVICE_DESIGN_BLUEPRINT.md:129`).

## 2. What the dashboard shows today

- **Period selection:** `periodId` overrides everything; otherwise it opens the current OPEN period, or the first period returned. Consequently, on 2026-10-28 it is not guaranteed to mean October 2026 (`src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:30`, `src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:53`). Opening-readings periods are excluded (`src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:42`).
- **Header/navigation:** organization, formatted month, OPEN/LOCKED badge, “Open workbench,” and previous/next period links (`src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:221`, `src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:246`).
- **Three KPIs:** “Total invoiced,” “Paid,” and “Outstanding,” displayed without explanatory hints (`src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:279`; hints are supported by `src/components/ui/KpiCard.astro:8`).
- **Needs attention:** count plus dwelling rows limited to `MISSING_DATA` and `OVERDUE`. Missing rows list blocker labels and offer “Add readings”; overdue rows say only that payment is past due and link to Payments (`src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:313`, `src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:326`, `src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:377`).
- **Case status:** seven linked counts—Missing data, Ready to invoice, Draft, Prepared, Overdue, Sent, Paid (`src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:109`, `src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:443`; labels at `src/lib/ui/invoice-status.ts:7`).
- **Consumption:** summed cold- and hot-water consumption in m³ (`src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:468`).
- **Setup only:** incomplete organizations get a five-step setup checklist; completed organizations get no comparable monthly progress cue (`src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:119`, `src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:151`).

## 3. Missing or misleading

### The financial KPIs do not form one equation

“Total invoiced” is actually only `Σ invoice.currentCharges`; it is not invoice `total` or `amountDue` (`src/domain/periods/dashboard.ts:126`, `src/domain/periods/dashboard.ts:156`). The query includes every generated invoice in the period without filtering out DRAFT or PREPARED, so “invoiced” does not mean “issued/sent” (`src/domain/periods/dashboard.ts:63`, `src/domain/periods/cases.ts:27`).

“Outstanding” is `Σ max(amountDue − net allocations, 0)`, while “Paid” is net allocations, with a `paidAt` fallback (`src/domain/periods/dashboard.ts:135`, `src/domain/periods/dashboard.ts:143`). Thus Paid + Outstanding normally reconciles to **amount due**, not “Total invoiced.” Carried debt, credit and late fees affect Outstanding insofar as they are already incorporated into `amountDue`; their separately calculated totals are never displayed (`src/domain/periods/dashboard.ts:127`, `src/domain/periods/dashboard.ts:220`).

KPIs silently exclude invoices whose snapshotted currency differs from the organization’s current currency, but case counts do not (`src/domain/periods/dashboard.ts:81`, `src/domain/periods/dashboard.ts:189`). The case read model also exposes `invoiceTotal`, `invoiceCurrentCharges`, `invoiceAmountDue` and carry-over separately, creating several plausible “total” definitions across screens (`src/domain/periods/cases.ts:67`).

### Operational gaps

- `MISSING_DATA` counts dwellings, not missing readings/inputs; one dwelling can contain several blockers (`src/domain/periods/dashboard.ts:204`, `src/domain/periods/case-readiness.ts:57`).
- READY cases are excluded from “Needs attention,” despite “Can I issue invoices?” being a primary dashboard branch (`src/domain/periods/dashboard.ts:45`, `docs/product/USER_JOURNEYS.md:24`).
- Overdue rows show neither amount nor due date, although those rows already contain `invoiceAmountDue` (`src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:377`, `src/domain/periods/cases.ts:69`).
- Delivery failures and unmatched/proposed payments are absent. The UI vocabulary supports `FAILED` and `UNMATCHED`, but the dashboard’s seven-status array omits them (`src/lib/ui/invoice-status.ts:27`, `src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:109`).
- There is no “N of X complete,” recommended next step, or monthly deadline.
- Raw water totals are weak dashboard content: they sum only readings already present, so incomplete collection lowers the total; there is no previous-month comparison or anomaly threshold (`src/domain/periods/dashboard.ts:158`, `src/domain/periods/dashboard.ts:178`). Consumption is useful as detail/history, or as an exception when materially unusual—not as two isolated totals.
- Seven equally weighted workflow rows are technically meaningful, but Draft versus Prepared requires process knowledge. Monitoring should foreground blockers and next actions; detailed states can remain as filters (`src/domain/periods/case-readiness.ts:2`, `docs/product/SERVICE_DESIGN_BLUEPRINT.md:99`).
