# Admin needs and content for a "current month" dashboard (Latvian house co-op / apartment-owners' association administrator)

Research date: 2026-10-28. Scope: Latvia only. Persona: ~50 y.o. volunteer or part-time chair/bookkeeper (priekšsēdētājs / grāmatvedis / pilnvarotā persona) of a small dzīvokļu īpašnieku kopība / biedrība / kooperatīvā sabiedrība (6-60 dwellings), Latvian only, Excel + paper background.

Evidence convention used below: "Cited Findings" = stated in a fetched or searched source (all of the legal-portal sources are lvportals.lv e-consultations, which state they are informational and not binding). "Inferences" = my reasoning, NOT verified. Primary law text on likumi.lv could not be fetched (connection refused from this environment), so statute article numbers are taken from lvportals.lv / vestnesis.lv summaries, not from the statute itself. Search summaries (WebSearch) are used only where flagged.

## 1. What is the actual monthly cycle in Latvian house management, and which laws/regulations apply?

### Takeaway
The monthly cycle is: owners read meters every month and pass readings to whoever calculates the payable share, the calculator splits supplier costs per dwelling by the key the owners/contract/regulation prescribe (ownership share, area, apartment count, residents, or meter), owners pay into the association/manager account, and the manager pays suppliers and keeps the books; once a year the manager reports in writing on funds used. The splitting key differs per cost type (there is explicitly "no single formula"), which is the main source of complexity and of conflicts.

### Cited Findings
**Roles and money flow**
- Manager (pārvaldnieks) keeps a bank account into which residents pay monthly; the manager pays utility providers from it and in contracts with suppliers acts for the owners' association, not in its own name. Association funds (utility payments, reserves, repair funds) are common property; a separate association account is described as the clearer legal/accounting approach. — [LV portāls e-consultation 39009](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/39009) (as summarised by search tool; page itself not fetched)
- Manager duties under Dzīvojamo māju pārvaldīšanas likums (DMPL) Art. 6: prepare the management work plan; give owners a report on what was done and funds used; prepare the annual budget draft (gada budžeta projekts) and organise financial accounting (finanšu uzskaite); if the contract says so, calculate owners' payments and organise settlements; keep the house file (mājas lieta); plan repairs, regular inspections, emergency fixes; convene meetings/surveys when owner decisions are needed. — [LV portāls guide "Daudzdzīvokļu mājas pārvaldīšana 2026"](https://lvportals.lv/skaidrojumi/393279-celvedis-daudzdzivoklu-majas-parvaldisana-2026)
- Payments owners owe include pārvaldīšanas maksa (management fee) and mājas uzkrājuma maksa (reserve/repair-fund contribution), split by domājamā daļa (ownership share) under Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums (DzĪL) Art. 13. The guide gives no minimum reserve amount or spending procedure. — [LV portāls guide 2026](https://lvportals.lv/skaidrojumi/393279-celvedis-daudzdzivoklu-majas-parvaldisana-2026)
- The kopība (owners' general meeting) is responsible for creating the uzkrājumu fonds and deciding how it is spent. — [LV portāls guide 2026](https://lvportals.lv/skaidrojumi/393279-celvedis-daudzdzivoklu-majas-parvaldisana-2026)

**Splitting keys (per cost type)**
- Management costs: default split "proporcionāli dzīvokļa īpašuma kopīpašuma domājamajām daļām" unless the kopība decides otherwise; for maintenance/common-area costs no fixed formula - it depends on service type, regulation, contract, owners' decisions; the manager must state the legal basis for each charge. — [LV portāls 38670](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/38670-normativajos-aktos-dzivojamas-majas-izdevumiem-nav-noteikta-viena-aprekina-formula-2026)
- Heating/hot water/circulation (e.g. Rīgas siltums): supplier delivers heat to the building entrance; splitting between heating, hot water and circulation is the manager's responsibility. To audit a bill, one asks for the supplier's total bill, heat-meter data, the hot-water formula, the circulation calculation and a sample monthly calculation for one apartment. — [LV portāls 38670](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/38670-normativajos-aktos-dzivojamas-majas-izdevumiem-nav-noteikta-viena-aprekina-formula-2026)
- MK noteikumi Nr. 524 (15.09.2015), "Kārtība, kādā nosaka, aprēķina un uzskaita katra dzīvojamās mājas īpašnieka maksājamo daļu..." covers water/sewerage, waste, and heating/hot water; methods depend on meter readings, floor area, number of residents, and heating-system type. — [Vēstnesis, MK 524](https://www.vestnesis.lv/ta/id/276739)
  - Pt. 2: owners read meters "katru mēnesi" (every month) and pass readings to the person calculating the payable share. Pts. 4-5, 15-16: waste and sewage may be split by apartment count, by ownership share, or by declared/actual residents; sewage in proportion to water used. Pts. 6-8: water measured at the building main meter; apartment meters or split by apartment count/share. Pts. 17-22: heat split into space heating, hot water, circulation; floor-area method per Annex 1. — [Vēstnesis, MK 524](https://www.vestnesis.lv/ta/id/276739) (fetch covered only the first ~100k of 435k characters)
  - No day-of-month deadline for readings in the part read; Pt. 11.1 refers to a deadline set by the owners ("noteiktajā termiņā"). — [Vēstnesis, MK 524](https://www.vestnesis.lv/ta/id/276739)
- Missing readings under MK 524 (owner-managed houses): if readings are late and no absence reported, consumption is estimated from the dwelling's average of the last 3 months, not for more than 3 months in a row; if missing >3 months the water difference is split among owners in proportion to the number of apartments. — [LV portāls 30168](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/30168-ja-nenodod-udens-skaititaja-radijumus-2023)
- Under MK Nr. 1013 (houses where owners have NOT taken over management; manager chooses method) the difference may be split by property count, last month's or 3-month average consumption, share, or registered residents. — [LV portāls 30168](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/30168-ja-nenodod-udens-skaititaja-radijumus-2023); the 1013 regulation text is at [likumi.lv/185342](https://m.likumi.lv/ta/id/185342) (not fetched).
- Water-consumption difference (ūdens patēriņa starpība): if the difference between the house meter and the sum of apartment meters exceeds 20% for three consecutive months and an owner has applied, the manager must find the cause and notify owners in writing within 2 months (MK 524 Pt. 14). Manager duties: check the main (commercial) meter, look for leaks between it and apartment meters, inspect and record apartment meters, use certified verification, keep acts. — [Vēstnesis, MK 524](https://www.vestnesis.lv/ta/id/276739); [LSM 2023 article](https://www.lsm.lv/raksts/dzive--stils/ikdienai/patereta-udens-starpiba-ka-tikt-gala-ar-pazudusu-udeni-daudzdzivoklu-maja.a491409/) (article is Jan 2023, may be dated)
- If an owner repeatedly blocks meter inspection or seals are broken, the manager may calculate that dwelling's water as if unmetered; if an error is found in settlement or heat calculation, a recalculation is made for the last settlement period. — [search-tool summary of MK 1013/524 context; secondary, unverified against text](https://m.likumi.lv/ta/id/185342)
- Individual water meters are the property of each owner, who is responsible for timely verification (verificēšana); a manager may buy/install/verify centrally on the owners' authorisation. — [LV portāls 33494 (search summary)](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/33494-udens-skaititaju-verificesana-2024)

**Reporting to owners (annual)**
- DMPL Art. 15(2): the manager must submit a written report at least once a year, per the procedure set in the management contract, including "pārskatu par pārvaldniekam nodoto finanšu līdzekļu un mantas izlietošanu"; no fixed calendar date in the law as summarised. Art. 14(2): on request the manager must provide information on how the management task is performed, including use of accumulated funds. Art. 16(2): manager is liable for non-compliance. — [LV portāls 36503](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/36503-tiesibas-sanemt-no-parvaldnieka-informaciju-par-lidzeklu-izlietosanu-2025)
- Management plan: manager presents the work plan in writing at least once a year (DMPL Art. 15). — [search-tool summary of DMPL, not text-verified](https://www.likumi.lv/ta/id/193573)
- House file (mājas lieta, DMPL Art. 8) must contain at least: management contract, kopība decisions and authorisations, management plans and estimates (tāmes), income-and-expense reports (ieņēmumu un izdevumu pārskati), reserve-fund information, readings of the common meters (kopējo skaitītāju rādījumi), inspection documents. Owners may inspect it (Art. 28(3)). It is maintained in BIS (Būvniecības informācijas sistēma). — [LV portāls guide 2026](https://lvportals.lv/skaidrojumi/393279-celvedis-daudzdzivoklu-majas-parvaldisana-2026); [LV portāls 34778](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/34778-ja-parvaldnieks-neuzrada-pakalpojuma-piegadataja-rekinus-2024)
- The "faktisko ieņēmumu un izdevumu pārskats" is a house-file document; the source says it covers a balance sheet, profit/loss calculation and bank statements; Art. 6 makes financial accounting mandatory; Grāmatvedības likums is mentioned only in the context of missing records. No deadlines stated. — [LV portāls 33189](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/33189-ko-nozime-faktisko-ienemumu-un-izdevumu-parskats-2024)
- Owner right to see what justifies charges: the owner may inspect information substantiating charges including supplier invoices; can check tariffs applied, calculation vs supplier invoices, and the split vs owners' decisions. Manager may decide the form (viewing or electronic copies) but cannot refuse specific information. — [LV portāls 38334](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/38334-dzivokla-ipasnieka-tiesibas-iepazities-ar-informaciju-par-aprekinatajiem-maksajumiem-2026)

**General meeting (kopsapulce)**
- Quorum: registered owners representing more than half of all dwellings; repeat meeting needs more than one third; decisions on management need more than half of all dwellings (e.g. 40 of 78); decisions can be in meeting, written survey, agreement or mixed, including electronically in BIS. — [LV portāls guide 2026](https://lvportals.lv/skaidrojumi/393279-celvedis-daudzdzivoklu-majas-parvaldisana-2026)
- Repeat meeting under DzĪL Art. 19.1 must be convened within one month; minutes within 14 days when convened via BIS. — [search-tool summary of DzĪL; unverified against text](https://m.likumi.lv/ta/id/361617)

**Arrears (parādi)**
- Each owner answers for their own obligations; the kopība does not become jointly liable for other owners' debts; one apartment's debt cannot be moved to other owners without legal basis. A write-off cannot be silently reversed; material corrections need owners' information and possibly a meeting decision. — [LV portāls 38418 (with association "Mājoklis")](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/38418-dzivoklu-ipasnieku-kopibai-nav-solidaras-atbildibas-par-citu-ipasnieku-paradiem-2026)
- Senate (court) position: the whole building is not liable for a neighbour's individual utility debts. — [LSM report](https://www.lsm.lv/raksts/zinas/latvija/senats-visa-daudzdzivoklu-maja-neatbild-par-kaiminu-individualajiem-komunalajiem-paradiem.a401600/)
- New DzĪL Art. 13.1 (buyer's liability for previous owner's payment arrears) in force from 1 January 2026 per a search-tool summary - [likumi.lv 361617 (unverified detail)](https://m.likumi.lv/ta/id/361617).
- Extensive DzĪL amendments took effect 29 May 2026 (headline: atsevišķās lietošanas tiesība, exclusive-use right over parts of common property, e.g. storage/parking). — [iFinanses](https://ifinanses.lv/bizness/raksti/uznemuma-vadiba/uznemuma-vadiba/biz-new-jauni-instrumenti-un-skaidraki-noteikumi-maju-parvaldisana/31115) (rest paywalled)

**Law in flux**
- Draft "Daudzdzīvokļu dzīvojamo māju pārvaldīšanas likums" (22-TA-3669) was still in inter-institutional review in mid-2026 per tapportals documents; a transitional provision gives management contracts until 1 June 2028 to be aligned; DMPL remains operative until it is replaced. — [TAP portāls 22-TA-3669](https://tapportals.mk.gov.lv/structuralizer/data/nodes/565b6d02-460b-4226-a4b9-32aa97166c09/preview.docx) (search-tool summary; adoption status unconfirmed)

### Inferences
- The admin's real monthly "job" is: (1) chase readings, (2) obtain supplier invoices, (3) apply a different split key per line, (4) issue a rēķins/paziņojums per dwelling, (5) reconcile payments, (6) pay suppliers, (7) keep documents ready for the owner who asks "why this amount?". A dashboard should mirror those seven steps in this order.
- Because every cost line may legitimately use a different key (ownership share vs area vs residents vs meters), "explain this number" (show the basis per line) is a first-class need, supported by the legal right of owners to see the basis and supplier invoices.
- The 20%/3-month water-difference trigger and the 3-month missing-reading rule are concrete, rule-based alerts a tool could compute automatically (in a house with a house meter). Whether the persona's houses have house meters is not established.
- The "15 October notification of next year's management payments" claim surfaced in one search-tool summary of likumi.lv/292334 ("Cabinet regulation on management payments") but I could not open the document and a second fetch of MK 524 did not show it; treat as UNVERIFIED and do not build around it without checking the regulation.
- Which regulation (524 vs 1013) applies depends on whether the owners have taken over management; for the persona (self-managed association / authorised person) MK 524 is likely the relevant one, but a house with an external manager under contract may fall under the contract or 1013. Needs legal confirmation.

### Gaps
- Primary text of DMPL, DzĪL, Grāmatvedības likums, MK 524 (beyond first ~100k chars), MK 1013 could not be read (likumi.lv connection refused; Vēstnesis only partial).
- No verified fixed calendar deadlines for readings, bill issuance, or payments in law (sources say owners/contract decide).
- Grāmatvedības likums obligations for a small biedrība/kooperatīvā sabiedrība (simplified vs full bookkeeping, annual report to VID/Lursoft for biedrības) were not researched; nor was the fiscal-year reporting duty of biedrības to the register.
- GDPR for resident data (names, readings, arrears lists shown to neighbours) - not researched; only the DMPL Art. 28(3) caveat ("respecting personal data protection rules") was found. See Inferences under Q3/Q4 for the design implication.
- No source confirms the persona's typical building size/composition (housing co-op "kooperatīvā sabiedrība" vs "biedrība" vs "kopība") in Latvian practice; the Kooperatīvo sabiedrību likums Art. 21 (members' right to information) is only mentioned in LV portāls 36503.

## 3. Pain points and failure modes of such admins

### Takeaway
Documented pain points concentrate on meters (late/missing readings, the unexplained water difference that gets split among everybody), on trust (owners demanding invoices and justification; managers accused of misusing collected money), and on debts (individual vs collective liability, unclear write-offs). Fear of mistakes and the need to explain numbers at the meeting are partly documented by the legal framework (owner rights, manager liability) but not by direct admin testimony.

### Cited Findings
- Late or false readings are named as a main cause of the water difference; a neighbour not submitting readings for years is described in a forum question with losses over 100 EUR per flat. — [search-tool summary of LSM/lvportals sources](https://www.lsm.lv/raksts/dzive--stils/ikdienai/patereta-udens-starpiba-ka-tikt-gala-ar-pazudusu-udeni-daudzdzivoklu-maja.a491409/); residents report proportional allocation raised their charges unusually high. — [LSM 2023](https://www.lsm.lv/raksts/dzive--stils/ikdienai/patereta-udens-starpiba-ka-tikt-gala-ar-pazudusu-udeni-daudzdzivoklu-maja.a491409/)
- Commentary reports managers using collected utility money for other purposes instead of paying providers, and that rules give no clear procedure for utility payments in transit. — [LV portāls opinion 254666 (search summary)](https://lvportals.lv/viedokli/254666-lai-godpratigam-dzivokla-ipasniekam-nav-jamaksa-divreiz-2013) (2013, old)
- Owners can sue or revoke the management task for failing duties; manager is liable (DMPL Art. 16(2)); owners can complain about a manager refusing to show house file. — [LV portāls 36503](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/36503-tiesibas-sanemt-no-parvaldnieka-informaciju-par-lidzeklu-izlietosanu-2025); [LV portāls 34778](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/34778-ja-parvaldnieks-neuzrada-pakalpojuma-piegadataja-rekinus-2024)
- A 2026 case: a municipal utility reversed an earlier debt write-off and re-added it to the building's debt without informing owners; the advice is that material financial corrections must not be hidden in annual reports. — [LV portāls 38418](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/38418-dzivoklu-ipasnieku-kopibai-nav-solidaras-atbildibas-par-citu-ipasnieku-paradiem-2026)
- Heat/hot-water splitting is a recurring dispute area; owners are told to ask for the total bill, meter data and a sample apartment calculation. — [LV portāls 38670](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/38670-normativajos-aktos-dzivojamas-majas-izdevumiem-nav-noteikta-viena-aprekina-formula-2026)
- Tenancy/ownership change: from 1 Jan 2026 a buyer's liability for the previous owner's arrears (DzĪL 13.1) exists. — [search-tool summary, unverified](https://m.likumi.lv/ta/id/361617)

### Inferences
- Probable failure modes for the persona (not directly sourced): forgetting to chase readings before the calculation date; supplier invoice arrives after the bills went out (so a recalculation/korekcija needed); wrong key used for one line; rounding mismatches vs supplier invoice; arrears conversations with neighbours using stale numbers; not knowing when the next annual report/kopsapulce is due; difficulty exporting a clean "report for owners".
- "Fear of mistakes" implies the dashboard should show quiet reassurance checks ("sums match the supplier invoice", "all N dwellings included") rather than only alarms; "needing to explain numbers" implies a one-click path from any total to its components and to the supplier invoice (the legal owner right in LV portāls 38334).
- GDPR (inference, not researched): showing arrears by name on a screen that may be shared at a meeting or viewed by another board member is a privacy risk; the DMPL Art. 28(3) caveat suggests redaction when exposing data. Default the dashboard to dwelling number + amount, with names one click away.

### Gaps
- No first-person testimony (forum posts, interviews) from volunteer chairs or bookkeepers was found; the Delfi/LV.lv forum searches returned lvportals/LSM/Senate material instead.
- No sourced statistics on how many small associations use Excel, or on arrears rates for small associations (an LSM 2023 article on "iedzīvotāju parādi par komunālajiem maksājumiem" exists but was not fetched).

## 4. What does the admin ask when opening the tool? Prioritized list and priority matrix

### Takeaway
Everything below is INFERENCE (UX synthesis from the legal cycle in Q1 and the repo's own journey). The dashboard for "current month" should answer five questions in order: where are we in this month's cycle, what is blocking, who owes, did the supplier-side money match, and what is due next. Everything else is one click away or kept off the dashboard.

### Cited Findings (anchors from the repo, not external)
- The repo's own admin journey states the dashboard's question as "What needs attention now?", with attention branches: Missing readings, Ready to bill, Overdue payment, and states Open/locked period. — `C:\Users\nesto\orca\projects\dzik118\docs\product\USER_JOURNEYS.md` (Admin journey, "Monitor" stage and flowchart)
- The product's billing lifecycle: collect readings -> generate -> verify/prepare -> deliver (email/paper) -> reconcile payments (exact/partial/overpayment) -> review period/ledger/audit history. — same file
- Easybilly review notes that Easybilly's "Home" has monthly navigation, needs-action area, building readiness, recent activity, collection summary, and that Namkopa already has dashboard totals, cases needing attention, payment views and a monthly workbench. — `C:\Users\nesto\orca\projects\dzik118\docs\research\EASYBILLY_REVIEW_2026-10-05.md` ("Financial overview" row, "Coverage log: Home")
- The same review says the persona/product defers budgets, supplier-document upload, and AI; the product has no supplier-invoice/budget module. — same file ("Budget/documents", "Defer" paragraph). This means the "pay suppliers / compare supplier invoice total" part of the Latvian monthly cycle is NOT represented in the product today.
- Easybilly review warns about "completion overclaims readiness" and recommends distinguishing configuration complete, billing ready, and ready to send. — same file ("Friction" #1)
- The repo docs do not contain a written persona description of the Latvian admin (age, tech skill) - they describe roles only. I found no repo source stating that persona.

### Inferences: question list and priority matrix

**Questions the admin asks (in the order of the month):**
1. Which month am I on, and what is the state of the cycle? (readings in / bills drafted / sent / money in)
2. Who has not submitted readings yet? (names/dwelling numbers, count of N of M)
3. Can I generate bills now, or is something blocking (missing reading, missing owner details, missing tariff)?
4. Did I send the bills / which are not delivered (email failed, paper not handed over)?
5. Who has paid, who has not, how much is overdue in total and by dwelling?
6. Does the sum I billed match what the suppliers charged me (and the water difference vs house meter)?
7. Do I have cash for supplier invoices coming due? (outside product scope today)
8. What is coming: kopsapulce date, annual report due, meter verification expiries, reserve fund balance.
9. What changed since last time (new payment, new reading, owner message)?

**Design implications (inference):**
- One dominant "next step" card, reading like a sentence in Latvian ("Trūkst 3 rādījumi no 24 - Ievadīt"), because the persona is not tech-savvy and the repo flow already assigns the dashboard the "what needs attention now?" role.
- Use the legal 3-month and 20% rules as built-in, explained alerts, not raw numbers.
- Keep the same vocabulary the user knows (rēķins, rādījumi, parāds, pārmaksa, uzkrājumu fonds, apsaimniekošanas maksa) and EUR with comma decimals, dd.mm.yyyy dates.
- Show reassurance ("viss kārtībā") states, not just problem lists, given the fear-of-mistakes inference.

- No user research (interviews, analytics) was available; the matrix is untested. Frequency and cost columns are judgement calls.
- Whether the target persona also pays suppliers from this tool (outside the current product) is not established.
