## Executive assessment

The dashboard has a sensible foundation, but it reads more like an internal billing system than a monthly overview for a non-technical administrator. The largest gains would come from plainer Latvian, larger operational text, clearer overdue semantics, and removing unnecessary density.

## 1. Visual hierarchy and information order

The eye hits:

1. The month, because the display heading reaches roughly 30–40 px.
2. The filled primary button.
3. The three large KPI values.
4. The small status badge and attention list.

That is broadly correct. The month, adjacent status, and one primary action form a strong top band; avoid adding more header actions. However, “Atvērt norēķinu darbvietu” makes the most visually prominent action unnecessarily technical (src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:248, src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:259, src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:263).

The current order—KPIs, attention, case status, consumption—is appropriate for an Excel-oriented administrator: totals first, exceptions second, breakdowns last (src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:306, src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:339). If attention items exist, give that section stronger visual priority, but do not move it above the monthly totals.

“Current month” is not guaranteed: the page selects the open period, otherwise the first returned period, without identifying it as current or historical (src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:53). Add “Aktīvais periods” or “Iepriekšējais periods” when appropriate.

There may also be excessive space between the header and period navigation: the parent uses `space-y-6`, while `.page-header` already has a 1.75rem bottom margin (src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:235, src/styles/global.css:636).

## 3. Density and sizing

Likewise, amber “Outstanding” implies a problem even when payment is not yet due (src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:327). Keep it neutral unless the amount is specifically overdue.

## 5. Accessibility

The skip link and focusable main destination are good (src/layouts/AdminLayout.astro:75, src/layouts/AdminLayout.astro:156). Disabled period controls are non-interactive spans, which is preferable to disabled links; `aria-disabled` is largely redundant but harmless (src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:285).

Each KPI renders an `h2`, making heading navigation announce three metrics as peer sections before the real “Jāizdara” section (src/components/ui/KpiCard.astro:24). A single KPI group heading plus `<dl>/<dt>/<dd>` would be cleaner.

Custom links define hover styling but no local `:focus-visible` treatment in the supplied CSS (src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:672, src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:728). Add a consistent 2–3 px focus outline with offset.

Two verified dead selector groups can be removed: `.dashboard-period-list` and `.dashboard-totals` match nothing in the template (src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:676, src/pages/admin/o/[orgId]/dashboard/index.astro:681).
